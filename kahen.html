Angler's Dream

Sanp the Fisherman is a work that is deeply loved and consistently supported by many fans. If you are Japanese, reading Sanpei the Fisherman again – with Sanp's Tohoku dialect that evokes a sense of nostalgia, the scenery of a mountain village in Akt Prefecture, and characters who truly love fishing passionately pursuing their hobby – will surely remind you of the joy you felt the very first time you caught a fish.
